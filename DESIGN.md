# Italian Home Furniture — Website Branding Brief

The  Italin brand has an **Arabic-first furniture catalogue look**. Warm off-white backgrounds, white panels, charcoal buttons, and bold Cairo typography create a quiet, structured interface. Real furniture photography provides most of the visual color. Reuse the company wordmark, palette, typography, and photo treatment to carry this identity across Italian Home Furniture websites.

## Typography and layout

- **Typeface.** Use Cairo with Tahoma and Arial as fallbacks. Use weight 400 for body text, 600 for buttons and controls, and 800 for prominent page and model titles. The project also loads weight 700. Use tabular numerals for aligned prices and figures.
- **Language and direction.** Default to Egyptian Arabic with `lang="ar-EG"` and `dir="rtl"`. Right-align Arabic content and isolate Latin model codes and other mixed-direction text where needed.
- **Components.** Use white surfaces, thin borders, and small corners. Buttons and inputs use a 4 px radius and a 44 px minimum height. A strong dark divider anchors page headings.
- **Responsive navigation.** For an internal tool, reuse the white right-hand sidebar and charcoal active item. On phones, put the menu in the top bar and stack content vertically. A customer-facing site can use navigation suited to its pages while keeping the same palette, typography, and component styling.
